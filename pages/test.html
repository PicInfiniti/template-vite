What is this page for?

A small component sandbox. Use it to try styles and interactions before adding them to your project.

Where do I make changes?

Edit this page in pages/test.html, its behavior in src/pages/test.js, and shared styles in src/assets/sass/.

Can I make it my own?

Absolutely. Replace the content, swap the colors, or remove the demos. This is a starting point for your ideas.
